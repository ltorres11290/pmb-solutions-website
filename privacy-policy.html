Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2, 2026

PMB-Solutions provides IT support, technical troubleshooting, network services, and related technology solutions for individuals and small businesses. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website or services:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Service request details
  • Device or system information you provide for troubleshooting
  • Messages or files you submit through our contact forms
  • Basic website analytics (non-identifying)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide IT support and services
  • Respond to inquiries and schedule appointments
  • Improve our website and service offerings
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Communicate with you about your service request

3. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. While no system is 100% secure, we take appropriate measures to reduce risk.

4. Sharing of Information

We do NOT sell your information.

We only share information when necessary to:

  • Provide services you request
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights or prevent fraud

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic analytics tools to understand site traffic. These tools do not identify you personally.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to your information
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion where applicable

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
