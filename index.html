Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to what our clients ask most.

Do you offer on-site or remote support?
We offer both. Remote support is available for software, configuration, and diagnostic issues and is often the fastest option. On-site visits are available throughout New York for hardware installations, camera setups, network deployments, and any issue that requires a physical presence.
What size businesses do you work with?
We work with small to mid-sized businesses as well as individual professionals, delivering a consistent, enterprise-level standard of IT services across every engagement.
How quickly can you respond to an emergency?
We prioritize responsiveness and reliability in every engagement, ensuring that critical issues are addressed promptly and handled with urgency.
Do you offer monthly managed IT support plans?
Our managed IT services provide ongoing system monitoring, maintenance, and security management to ensure your environment remains stable, secure, and up to date.
Can you install security cameras and connect them to my network?
Yes. We manage the full lifecycle of camera system implementation, including physical installation, system configuration, network integration, and overall deployment.
What is your pricing structure?
Our services are structured based on the scope, complexity, and requirements of each engagement. We provide tailored service proposals following an initial consultation, allowing us to align the appropriate level of support, resources, and configuration to your specific needs. This ensures a clear, well-defined approach that supports both immediate requirements and long-term objectives. For more information or to discuss your requirements, we encourage you to reach out directly.
