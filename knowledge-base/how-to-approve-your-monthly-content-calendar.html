How to approve your monthly content calendar

Review efficiently and keep publishing timelines on track.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Review the calendar for dates, campaigns, and key promotions.
  2. Check captions for tone, clarity, and compliance needs.
  3. Confirm offers, links, and call-to-actions are accurate.
  4. Add revisions in one consolidated feedback pass.
  5. Submit final approval before the scheduled publish window.

Tips

  • Flag any blackout dates or holiday constraints in advance.
  • Keep feedback specific to reduce revision rounds.
  • Approve early to protect design and scheduling lead time.
