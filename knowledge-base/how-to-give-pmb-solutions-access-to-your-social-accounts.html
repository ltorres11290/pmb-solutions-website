How to give PMB-Solutions access to your social accounts

Grant secure role-based access without sharing passwords.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open your platform business manager or page settings.
  2. Add PMB-Solutions as a partner or user by email/business ID.
  3. Assign appropriate roles (editor, analyst, advertiser).
  4. Enable two-factor authentication on admin accounts.
  5. Confirm access by asking PMB-Solutions to verify permissions.

Tips

  • Never share personal account passwords directly.
  • Use least-privilege roles where possible.
  • Remove unused admin users regularly.
