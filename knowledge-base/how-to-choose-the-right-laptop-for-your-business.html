How to choose the right laptop for your business

Match hardware specs to your team workflows and budget.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Define primary use cases: admin, design, field work, or heavy apps.
  2. Choose at least 16GB RAM and SSD storage for business reliability.
  3. Prioritize battery life, durability, and warranty support options.
  4. Confirm compatibility with your software, VPN, and security tools.
  5. Standardize models for easier support and inventory control.

Tips

  • Business-class devices usually include better support terms.
  • Buy docks and accessories at the same time for consistency.
  • Plan a 3-4 year refresh cycle.
