How to access your booking system

Log in, manage availability, and keep bookings accurate.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open your booking platform URL provided during setup.
  2. Log in with your admin email credentials.
  3. Verify your business hours, service durations, and buffers.
  4. Review appointment notifications and calendar sync settings.
  5. Test a sample booking from the customer view.

Tips

  • Assign team roles to avoid accidental setting changes.
  • Keep cancellation and reminder rules clearly configured.
  • Check integrations after updates.
