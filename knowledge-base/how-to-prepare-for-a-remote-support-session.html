How to prepare for a remote support session

A short checklist to make your support session faster and more productive.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Confirm your device is powered on and connected to the internet.
  2. Close sensitive files and save any active work.
  3. Have your login credentials ready if access prompts appear.
  4. Open your email or text for the remote-session link from PMB-Solutions.
  5. Stay available during the session for approval prompts and quick questions.

Tips

  • Use a stable wired or strong WiFi connection.
  • Write down the main issue and when it started.
  • Share screenshots of errors in advance if possible.
