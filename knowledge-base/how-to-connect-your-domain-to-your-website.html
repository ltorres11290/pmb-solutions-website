How to connect your domain to your website

Basic DNS steps to point your domain to a live site safely.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Log into your domain registrar account.
  2. Locate DNS settings for the domain.
  3. Add or update A/CNAME records provided by your host.
  4. Verify SSL/certificate settings after DNS updates.
  5. Wait for propagation and test site loading on multiple devices.

Tips

  • Take screenshots before changing existing records.
  • Lower TTL before migrations for faster cutovers.
  • Keep a rollback note with previous DNS values.
