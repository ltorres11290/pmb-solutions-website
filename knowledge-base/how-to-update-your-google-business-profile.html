How to update your Google Business Profile

Keep your local listing accurate and visible to customers.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Sign into your Google Business Profile manager account.
  2. Update hours, phone, website, and service areas.
  3. Add fresh photos and recent business updates.
  4. Respond to reviews professionally and consistently.
  5. Monitor performance insights monthly for trend changes.

Tips

  • Use matching contact details across all directories.
  • Post seasonal updates to keep engagement active.
  • Avoid keyword stuffing in business descriptions.
