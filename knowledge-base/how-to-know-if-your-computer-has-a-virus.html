How to know if your computer has a virus

Common warning signs and immediate actions to reduce risk.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Watch for sudden slowdowns, pop-ups, and unknown apps.
  2. Run a full scan with reputable antivirus software.
  3. Disconnect from public networks if suspicious activity appears.
  4. Uninstall recently added unknown extensions or software.
  5. Contact PMB-Solutions for professional malware cleanup.

Tips

  • Keep your OS and antivirus signatures updated.
  • Avoid opening unexpected links and attachments.
  • Use multi-factor authentication on critical accounts.
