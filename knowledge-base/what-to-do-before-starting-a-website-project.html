What to do before starting a website project

Prepare key information early to shorten project timelines.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Define your primary goals: leads, sales, bookings, or awareness.
  2. Collect brand assets, service details, and business contact data.
  3. Review competitor websites for reference ideas.
  4. Identify required pages and must-have functionality.
  5. Assign one project contact for faster approvals.

Tips

  • Use a shared folder for all project files.
  • Prioritize clarity over volume for homepage messaging.
  • Share deadlines for launches or promotions early.
