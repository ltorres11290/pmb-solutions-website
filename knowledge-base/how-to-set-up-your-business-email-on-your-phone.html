How to set up your business email on your phone

Connect your mailbox securely for mobile productivity.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Install the recommended mail app (Outlook/Gmail/native app).
  2. Add your business email address and password.
  3. Complete multi-factor authentication if prompted.
  4. Allow calendar and contacts sync as needed.
  5. Send a test email to confirm outbound and inbound delivery.

Tips

  • Use app-level PIN or biometric lock for security.
  • Enable remote wipe for managed business devices.
  • Keep mobile OS updated for compatibility.
